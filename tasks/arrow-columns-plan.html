The change lands in 6 steps; each step ends with all tests green.

1 · Types

DataType, TypeTraits, VisitType. BYTE_ARRAY and FLBA mapping. A sed edits most of 327 test tags; Binary/Int96/FLBA tests change by hand.

2 · Buffers

The largest step. A final ColumnVector breaks every subclass, so all producers move at once: reader, generate, expression results, literals, INT96, and approximately 140 test lines. GetValue stays as an adapter.

3 · Batch kernels

Filter, gather, concatenate, spill, size_in_bytes.

4 · Expressions

Cast, compare, arithmetic, CASE read views, not GetValue.

5 · Operators

Hash join, hash aggregate, sort. src/ stops calling GetValue.

6 · Scalar

Scalar literals; tests use Get<T>. Value goes.

Q12 at scale factor 1 must run in less time than 845 ms.
